# Privacy

My Copilot Framework is a set of templates and instructions. It contains no code that collects, sends or stores data; your framework files stay wherever you put them.

- Never publish a populated personal copy. Keep your working copy private.
- Store your working copy only in a location your organisation approves, and follow its privacy, classification, retention and acceptable-use requirements.
- Configure only knowledge sources you are authorised to use.

To report a problem with this repository, please open an issue. Do not include personal or confidential information in issues.
